FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know before booking your appointment with us.

Do I need an appointment?

While we welcome walk-ins based on availability, booking in advance ensures you secure your preferred time slot and stylist.

Do you offer hair colouring?

Yes, we offer a full range of hair coloring services, including root touch-ups, highlights, balayage, and custom global shades.

Do you offer bridal makeup?

Yes! We provide specialized bridal and party makeup packages. We recommend consulting with our artists prior to your special event.

What products do you use?

We use professional, salon-grade hair and skincare products formulated to protect your hair integrity and skin health.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday through Sunday from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM to accommodate your schedule.
